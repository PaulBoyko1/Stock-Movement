Interpret the baseline narrowly

12–1 momentum uses the prior 12-month window excluding the most recent month. The 10 bp cost is a proportional allocation-change approximation, not a measured execution cost. Underlying portfolio implementation costs, point-in-time ETF availability and executable fills are not established by these reconstructed series.

The universe is 12 research industries, without a liquid-stock eligibility filter. Equal weighting refers to the allocation across industries; the stocks within each supplied industry portfolio are value weighted.

This fixed snapshot reports month-end drawdowns only. A live or daily implementation could experience larger losses between observations. The smaller full-period drawdown does not establish general safety: in the 2010s the strategy’s month-end drawdown was −23.19%, versus −17.51% for the benchmark. Advanced mode exposes assumptions; it does not turn missing validation into evidence.

E04-P2 · same 312 months · primary 10 bp assumed allocation cost

INTERVAL INCLUDES ZERO
+0.29%Annualized arithmetic alpha
Approximate 95% interval: −2.67% to +3.24%. These historical data do not establish positive factor-adjusted alpha.

Contemporaneous attribution to market, size, value, profitability, investment and stock momentum. Alpha is the monthly regression intercept ×12; it is not a forecast or CAGR difference.

Diagnostic details

OLS with an intercept and six factors; Bartlett Newey–West covariance, 12 lags, n/(n−7) correction, normal interval. R² = 0.430 describes historical active-return variation explained, not forecasting accuracy or the share of mean return explained. Stock-momentum exposure = 0.3455 (HAC standard error 0.0356).

Constant exposures, omitted factors, reconstructed portfolios and approximate costs limit interpretation. No signal settings changed after this result.
